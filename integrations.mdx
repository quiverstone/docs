## Planned integrations

Integrations currently on the roadmap include:

- **Identity providers** — SSO via SAML and OIDC, plus SCIM user and group provisioning (Azure AD, Okta, Google Workspace).
- **Communication tools** — Slack and Microsoft Teams for notifications, approval flows, and activity digests.
- **Incident management** — PagerDuty and Opsgenie for on-call context and break-glass workflows.
- **ITSM** — ServiceNow and Jira integration for ticket-driven access reviews and approvals.
- **SIEM / observability** — structured event streams for Splunk, Datadog, and other log aggregators.

## Available today

For customers who need to start building automation against Quiverstone before the integrations catalog ships, [contact support](mailto:support@quiverstone.io) to discuss early access and custom integration options.
