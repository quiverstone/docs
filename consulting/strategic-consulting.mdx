Get the clarity you need to move forward.

In the rapidly evolving AWS ecosystem, decision paralysis is real. Quiverstone’s Strategic Consulting service is designed to cut through the noise. We provide high-level architectural guidance, "second opinions" on proposed infrastructure changes, and tactical problem-solving for teams that need expert validation before writing code.

## **Core Offerings**

### **1. Architectural Reviews**

Before you commit to a multi-year reserved instance plan or a complex refactor, let us validate your approach.

- **Gap Analysis:** We review your current diagrams against AWS Well-Architected Framework pillars.
- **Cost Optimization:** Analysis of underutilized resources and recommendations for Savings Plans or Spot Instances.
- **Security Posture:** High-level review of IAM policies, public-facing endpoints, and encryption standards.

### **2. Ad-Hoc Technical Advisory**

Sometimes you don't need a full project team; you just need a Senior Architect for an hour.

- **"Unstick" Your Team:** Real-time troubleshooting for blockers during deployments.
- **Tool Selection:** Unbiased advice on choosing between AWS native tools (e.g., CodePipeline) vs. third-party solutions (e.g., GitHub Actions, GitLab).
- **Compliance Interpretation:** Translating SOC 2, HIPAA, or ISO requirements into technical AWS configurations.

## **Typical Engagement Model**

**Hourly Advisory (\$200/hr)** This service is typically consumed via our **Hourly Advisory** model. It requires no long-term contracts and is strictly Time & Materials.

- **Communication:** Slack/Teams integration or Video Calls.
- **Deliverables:** Written summary of recommendations, architectural diagrams (Draw.io/LucidChart), and links to relevant documentation.
