title: "Project Based"
---

Best for: Defined scopes with a clear beginning and end. (e.g., Migrations, New Landing Zones, AI Stack Deployment).

- **Example Engagement:** \$2,500
- **Structure:** Fixed Fee or Not-to-Exceed (NTE) Time & Materials.
- **Payment Schedule:** typically 50% upfront, 50% upon completion/acceptance.
- **The Process:**
  1. **Discovery:** We map out your requirements.
  2. **SOW (Statement of Work):** We provide a document outlining exact deliverables, timeline, and acceptance criteria.
  3. **Execution:** We build in your environment.
  4. **Handoff:** We transfer code repos, documentation, and conduct a training session.
