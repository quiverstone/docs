# Teams

<Note>
**Status:** In Development - This feature is currently under development and will be available in a future release.
</Note>

## Overview

Teams in Quiverstone will provide a higher-level organization structure for collaborative account management. Teams combine users, groups, and permissions into cohesive units for managing AWS accounts.

## Planned Features

### Team Structure
- Create multi-user teams
- Add groups to teams
- Assign team leads and members
- Define team hierarchies

### Team-Based Access
- Grant teams access to organizations
- Assign teams to customer accounts
- Apply team-level role permissions
- Inherit permissions from organization assignments

### Team Collaboration
- Shared dashboards and views
- Team activity feeds
- Collaborative troubleshooting
- Team-based notifications

## Planned Team Types

**Customer Service Teams**
- Dedicated to specific customers
- Access to customer organizations and accounts
- Team-specific role configurations

**Project Teams**
- Temporary teams for specific projects
- Time-limited access grants
- Project completion workflows

**On-Call Teams**
- Rotating team membership
- Emergency access permissions
- Escalation workflows

**Service Delivery Teams**
- Organized by service offering (Migration, Security, Optimization)
- Service-specific access patterns
- Cross-customer access based on service type

## Use Cases

**For MSPs:**
- Create customer-dedicated service teams
- Organize staff by specialization (AWS, Security, Networking)
- Manage contractor and employee access separately
- Implement follow-the-sun support with regional teams

**For Enterprises:**
- Create business unit teams
- Organize by application or service ownership
- Implement platform team structures
- Support matrix organization models

**For Consulting Firms:**
- Create engagement-based teams
- Manage consultant assignments to customers
- Time-limited access for project duration
- Knowledge transfer and handoff workflows

## Integration Plans

- **Communication Tools** - Slack, Microsoft Teams integration
- **Project Management** - JIRA, ServiceNow integration
- **Incident Management** - PagerDuty, Opsgenie integration
- **Calendar Integration** - Team schedules and on-call rotations
