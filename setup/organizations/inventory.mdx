# Deploy Organization Inventory Roles

To enable Quiverstone to discover and inventory your AWS Organization's member accounts, deploy an IAM role in your management or delegated administrator account. Choose the deployment method that best fits your security requirements.

## Deployment Options

### Direct Access (Recommended)
Quiverstone production account directly assumes a role in your management account. **Simplest and most common deployment.**

### Chained Access
Quiverstone assumes a role in an intermediate account you control, which then assumes the target role. **Use when security policies require an additional layer.**

## Direct Access Deployment

**Best for:** Most organizations, quickest setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch the CloudFormation Template">
    <Note>
    **Important:** Ensure you're logged into the correct AWS account in your browser before clicking the launch button.
    </Note>

    Click the button below to deploy the role in your AWS management account:

    [![Launch Stack](/assets/stack.png)](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudformation/home?region=us-east-1#/stacks/quickcreate?templateURL=https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/assets.quiverstone.io/role-catalog/inventory/quiverstone-direct-org-inventory.yaml&stackName=quiverstone-direct-org-inventory)

    **Deploy To:** Management Account or Delegated Admin Account (in us-east-1)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Parameters">
    - **Role Name:** Leave default (`QuiverstoneDirectOrgInventory`) or customize
    - **Quiverstone Account ID:** Leave as `684035162433` (do not modify)
    - **External ID:** Leave empty for now (you'll get this from Quiverstone)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Stack">
    1. Check the IAM capabilities acknowledgment box
    2. Click **Create stack**
    3. Wait for deployment to complete (typically 1-2 minutes)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the Role ARN">
    1. Navigate to **Outputs** tab
    2. Copy the **TargetRoleArn** value
    3. Save this for Quiverstone configuration
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure in Quiverstone">
    1. In Quiverstone, navigate to **Organizations > Add Organization**
    2. Fill in organization details
    3. In the **Inventory Role** step:
       - **Role Type:** Select **DIRECT**
       - **Target Role ARN:** Paste the ARN from step 4
       - **External ID:** Enter the external ID provided by Quiverstone
       - **Session Name:** (optional) Enter `Quiverstone` for CloudTrail tracking
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you configured an External ID, you'll need to update the CloudFormation stack with the External ID value provided by Quiverstone.
</Note>

## Chained Access Deployment

**Best for:** Organizations requiring an additional security layer or compliance controls

Chained access creates a two-step role assumption path:
1. Quiverstone → Your intermediate account → Management account

### When to Use Chained Access
- Security policies prohibit direct external account trust
- Compliance requirements mandate intermediate controls
- You want centralized audit of all access through a trusted account
- You have existing intermediate role infrastructure

### Prerequisites
- An AWS account to serve as the intermediate/trusted account
- IAM permissions to deploy CloudFormation in both accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy Intermediate Role">
    <Note>
    **Important:** Log into your **intermediate/trusted account** in your browser before clicking the launch button.
    </Note>

    Click to deploy the intermediate role in your trusted account:

    [![Launch Stack](/assets/stack.png)](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudformation/home?region=us-east-1#/stacks/quickcreate?templateURL=https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/assets.quiverstone.io/role-catalog/inventory/quiverstone-chained-org-inventory-intermediate.yaml&stackName=quiverstone-chained-org-inventory-intermediate)

    **Parameters:**
    - **Role Name:** Leave default or customize
    - **Destination Account ID:** Enter your management account ID
    - **Destination Role Name:** Leave default (`QuiverstoneChainedOrgInventoryDestination`)
    - **Quiverstone Account ID:** Leave as `684035162433`
    - **External ID:** Leave empty for now
    - **Assume Role Scope:** Leave as `any-role-any-account` (recommended)

    Copy the **IntermediateRoleArn** from the Outputs tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deploy Destination Role">
    <Note>
    **Important:** Log into your **management account** in your browser before clicking the launch button.
    </Note>

    Click to deploy the destination role in your management account:

    [![Launch Stack](/assets/stack.png)](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudformation/home?region=us-east-1#/stacks/quickcreate?templateURL=https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/assets.quiverstone.io/role-catalog/inventory/quiverstone-chained-org-inventory-destination.yaml&stackName=quiverstone-chained-org-inventory-destination)

    **Parameters:**
    - **Role Name:** Must match the name used in Step 1 (default is fine)
    - **Trust Principals:** Select **Named** (trusts specific role)
    - **Intermediate Account ID:** Enter your intermediate account ID
    - **Intermediate Role Name:** Must match role name from Step 1
    - **External ID:** Leave empty for now

    Copy both **TargetRoleArn** and **IntermediateRoleArn** from Outputs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure in Quiverstone">
    In Quiverstone, navigate to **Organizations > Add Organization**:

    **Organization Info:**
    - Fill in organization details as usual

    **Inventory Role:**
    - **Role Type:** Select **CHAINED**
    - **Target Role ARN:** Paste destination role ARN
    - **Target External ID:** Enter external ID (if provided by Quiverstone)
    - **Target Session Name:** (optional) `Quiverstone`
    - **Intermediate Role ARN:** Paste intermediate role ARN
    - **Intermediate External ID:** Enter external ID (if using)
    - **Intermediate Session Name:** (optional) `Quiverstone-Chain`
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If using an existing intermediate role, deploy only the destination template and set **Trust Principals** to **All** to trust the entire intermediate account.
</Note>

## What Permissions Are Granted?

All inventory roles provide **read-only** access to:

**AWS Organizations:**
- Organization structure and hierarchy
- Organizational units and accounts
- Service Control Policies (SCPs)
- Delegated administrator details
- Account creation status

**Account Information:**
- Account contact details
- Available AWS regions

No write, modify, or delete permissions are granted.

## Updating External IDs

If you need to add or update an External ID after initial deployment:

1. Navigate to **CloudFormation** in the AWS Console
2. Select the stack (`quiverstone-direct-org-inventory` or `quiverstone-chained-org-inventory-*`)
3. Click **Update**
4. Select **Use current template**
5. Update the **pExternalId** parameter
6. Complete the update

Then update the configuration in Quiverstone.

## Troubleshooting

**Stack creation fails with IAM permissions error:**
- Ensure you have `iam:CreateRole` and `iam:PutRolePolicy` permissions
- Deploy in the correct account (management or delegated admin)

**Quiverstone cannot assume the role:**
- Verify the role ARN is correct
- Check External ID matches in both AWS and Quiverstone
- For chained access, ensure intermediate role can assume destination role
- Verify Quiverstone production account ID is `684035162433`

**Organization inventory not appearing:**
- Allow 5-10 minutes for initial inventory collection
- Check inventory status shows "ACTIVE" in Quiverstone
- Verify the role has the required Organizations permissions

## Security Best Practices

- **Use External IDs:** Always configure an external ID for additional security
- **Use CloudTrail:** Monitor role assumptions via CloudTrail logs
- **Least Privilege:** Roles grant only read permissions required for inventory
- **Regular Review:** Periodically review who has access and audit logs
- **Session Names:** Use descriptive session names for easier CloudTrail analysis

## Advanced Configuration

### Service Catalog Deployment

For MSPs managing multiple customer organizations, consider deploying the **Inventory Roles Portfolio** via AWS Service Catalog. This enables customers to self-service deploy inventory roles.

See [Service Catalog Setup](/setup/service-catalog) for details.

### Multiple Organizations

To manage multiple organizations:
1. Deploy the inventory role in each organization's management account
2. Add each organization separately in Quiverstone
3. Use consistent naming conventions (e.g., `QuiverstoneOrgInventory-CustomerName`)

## Next Steps

After deploying the inventory role and configuring the organization in Quiverstone:

1. **Verify Inventory:** Check that member accounts appear in **Organizations > View > Associated Accounts**
2. **Configure Access Roles:** Set up [account access roles](/setup/accounts/access) for individual account access
3. **Monitor Status:** Ensure inventory status remains "ACTIVE"
